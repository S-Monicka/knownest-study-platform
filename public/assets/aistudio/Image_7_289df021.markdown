## Brand & Style

This design system sets a focused, encouraging, and vibrant tone tailored for modern students. Balancing scholastic rigor with energizing playfulness, the visual language avoids both institutional sterility and juvenile distraction. 

The aesthetic is Modern Tactile Minimalism: crisp optical clarity, expansive whitespace, warm slate foundations, and hyper-saturated semantic status accents. Interfaces feel approachable and lightweight, celebrating student momentum, cognitive clarity, and daily streaks through clean card elevations, crisp typography, and deliberate bursts of motivational color.

## Colors

The palette leverages a crisp, high-clarity light mode engineered for long reading sessions and rapid visual scanning.

- **Primary (`#2563eb`)**: Energetic royal blue representing primary actions, focus states, and foundational brand moments.
- **Secondary (`#8b5cf6`)**: Vibrant electric purple reserved for achievements, mastery sprints, and creative learning modules.
- **Tertiary / Success (`#10b981`)**: Radiant emerald signaling mastered subjects, completed study sets, and positive performance deltas.
- **Warning & In-Progress (`#f59e0b`)**: Warm amber allocated to spaced-repetition schedules, items currently under review, and active streak warnings.
- **Danger & Attention (`#f43f5e`)**: Dynamic coral-rose indicating difficult topics, weak retention scores, and high-priority flashcard reviews.
- **Surface Hierarchy**: Base canvas is hosted on `#f8fafc` (Slate 50), stepping up to `#f1f5f9` (Slate 100) for inset wells, segmented bars, and sidebar rails. Interactive containers, study decks, and task cards sit on pure `#ffffff`.
- **Text & Contrast**: High-impact `#0f172a` for primary headings, `#1e293b` for primary body copy, and `#64748b` for instructional meta labels, ensuring strict WCAG AAA conformance across all daylight conditions.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a geometric, balanced, and approachable feel for headings and readable body text, avoiding visual fatigue during prolonged study sessions. 

**Space Grotesk** is introduced for status badges, tags, metrics, card metadata, and flashcard counters. Its technical, structured glyphs give actionable study telemetry (accuracy rates, deck counts, spaced repetition timers) an authoritative yet modern contrast.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a standard 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by an 8pt architectural rhythm. 

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: Max-width content boundaries lock at 1280px with 2.5rem (`margin`) canvas gutters and 1.5rem (`gutter`) inter-column spacing. Study dashboards favor asymmetrical 8/4 splits (primary learning content on the left, telemetry and study queue rails on the right).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with 2rem margins and 1.25rem gutters. Persistent sidebars collapse into drawer navigation or bottom action sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 1rem (`margin-mobile`) outer padding and 1rem (`gutter-mobile`) column gaps. Cards stretch full width with internal padding bounded by `space-md`. Sticky bottom navigation and progress bars anchor active study sessions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp boundaries paired with soft, low-contrast ambient shadows to keep the UI light and paper-clean:

- **Level 0 (Flat/Substrate)**: Canvas background `#f8fafc`. Secondary tracks and inactive drop zones sit flush on `#f1f5f9`.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Modules)**: Pure white `#ffffff` elevated by a structural border `1px solid #e2e8f0` and an ultra-soft dual shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Controls)**: Subtle upward movement (translateY -2px) with `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` and border shift to `#cbd5e1`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Active Study Decks)**: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` with border `#e2e8f0`. Backdrops employ a warm blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an open, friendly curvature profile (`roundedness: 2`). Standard controls, text fields, and list units utilize `0.5rem` (8px). Medium containers, cards, and modal dialogs extend into `1rem` (16px) and `1.5rem` (24px) corner radii (`rounded-xl` and `rounded-2xl`). 

Pill shapes (`9999px`) are exclusively used for status chips, micro-indicators, and floating study action timers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#2563eb` fill with `#ffffff` text, font `label-lg`. Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`, rounded-xl (1rem). Subtle drop shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.25)`. Hover: `#1d4ed8`.
- **Secondary / Action**: Tinted `#eff6ff` background, `#2563eb` text, border `1px solid #bfdbfe`.
- **Accent / Review**: Solid fills mapped to state (e.g., `#10b981` Mastered, `#f43f5e` Needs Review) with corresponding 20% alpha glow on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- Built with `label-md` uppercase typography. Height is fixed at 28px with full pill rounding (`9999px`) and horizontal padding of `0.75rem`.
- **Mastered**: Background `#ecfdf5`, text `#065f46`, border `1px solid #a7f3d0`.
- **Reviewing**: Background `#fffbeb`, text `#92400e`, border `1px solid #fde68a`.
- **Needs Review**: Background `#fff1f2`, text `#9f1239`, border `1px solid #fecdd3`.

### Cards & Study Containers
- Constructed on `#ffffff` with a crisp `1px solid #e2e8f0` outline and Level 1 elevation. Border-radius is standard `1rem` (or `1.5rem` for hero flashcard frames). Inner padding uses `1.5rem`. Top card headers feature a dedicated accent stripe or inline status chip.

### Input Fields
- Background `#ffffff` with `1px solid #cbd5e1`, inner padding `0.75rem 1rem`, rounded-xl (1rem). 
- Active focus state: border shifts to `#2563eb` accompanied by a distinct focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`. Placeholder text sits at `#94a3b8`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 20px squares/circles with `1.5px solid #cbd5e1` resting borders. Checked state triggers animated scale fill to `#2563eb` displaying a sharp white check/dot icon. Focus renders a 3px ring in `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`.

### Lists & Queue Items
- Grouped list rows rest on `#ffffff` borders or subtle dividers (`1px solid #f1f5f9`). Alternating hover states trigger `#f8fafc` wash. Drag handles and reorder indicators utilize `#94a3b8`.

### Specialized Learning Components
- **Flashcard Deck Module**: High-tactile front-and-back container using 3D perspective flip, Level 2 elevation on hover, and an ambient footer housing four quick-grade buttons (Again, Hard, Good, Easy) paired to the semantic accent colors.
- **Mastery Meter**: Segmented progress bar using a `#f1f5f9` rail with proportional fills of Emerald (`#10b981`), Amber (`#f59e0b`), and Coral (`#f43f5e`), height 8px with full pill rounding.
